# Synthetic data spec (Data 1)

Generated by `data/plantdata/`, deterministic by seed. Run from `data/`:

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
python -m plantdata --seed 42 --out out --copy-to ../twin/public/data
```

Time is **seconds since shift start** (`t_s`). A shift is 8 h = 28,800 s. Shift A starts 06:00, B starts 14:00. Space is **metres**: x east, z south, y up, origin at the plant's NW corner (same axes as Three.js, so `(x, z)` is the floor position).

| File | What | Committed |
|------|------|-----------|
| `out/layout.json` | Plant size, 12 stations, 11 machines, conveyor path, item flow, safety zones, pedestrian walk graph | yes |
| `out/employees.csv` | 24 employees (2 shifts x 12) | yes |
| `out/tasks.csv` | Task catalogue with mean/std durations | yes |
| `out/transfer_times.csv` | Item moves between stations and walking times between all station pairs | yes |
| `out/events.json` | The scripted hero fault and response timeline | yes |
| `out/schedule.json` | Per-person timelines for both shifts (drives the 3D playback) | yes |
| `out/sensors_shiftA_fault.parquet` | 1 Hz sensors, 10 machines, 8 h, with the M05 bearing-wear fault | no, regenerate |
| `out/sensors_healthy_train.parquet` | Same, no fault, different seed (train anomaly detection here) | no, regenerate |

## layout.json
`stations[]`: `id, name, x, z, w, d, access:[x,z], machines[]`. `access` is where people stand. `machines[]`: `id, name, type, station, x, z, rot_deg`. `walk_graph`: `nodes[{id,x,z}]` and `edges[[a,b]]`; people only walk along edges. `safety_zones[]`: rectangles around weld cells S05 and S06 (robot pauses visually if a person is inside). `item_flow[]`: `from, to, mode` (conveyor or agv).

Station and machine IDs are the contract with the graph owner (see DECISIONS.md D-008).

## employees.csv
`employee_id` (e.g. `A-OP04`), `name` (synthetic), `role` (operator, relief_operator, inspector, supervisor, technician), `shift`, `shift_start`, `home_station`, `skill_level` 1 to 5, `experience_years`, `certified_stations` (comma list), `task_speed_factor` (below 1 is faster), `walk_speed_mps`.

Per shift: 8 operators (S02, S04, S05, S06 x2, S07, S08, S09), 1 relief operator who covers breaks, 1 inspector, 1 supervisor, 1 technician.

## tasks.csv
`task_id, name, station, role_required, mean_s, std_s, trigger, period_s_or_n`. Triggers: `cycle` (once per takt, 90 s), `every_n_cycles` (fetch trips), `periodic`, `event` (bearing repair), `scheduled` (breaks). Actual durations are lognormal around the mean, scaled by the person's `task_speed_factor`.

## transfer_times.csv
`kind` (`item` or `walk`), `from_station, to_station, mode, distance_m, mean_s, std_s`. Conveyor 0.25 m/s, AGV 1.2 m/s plus 25 s handling, walking 1.3 m/s.

## events.json (hero scenario)
Weld robot 2 (`M05`), component `m05_joint1_bearing`, shift A. Onset at t = 3.0 h, vibration reaches 2x baseline (alert) at about t = 5.12 h, technician dispatched 5 min later, picks the bearing from S12, arrives at S06 at about 5.26 h and finishes the repair 25 min later; the wear then resets. The alert time is analytic, so all files agree.

## schedule.json
`shifts.A|B[]` -> `{employee_id, name, role, shift, home_station, timeline[]}`. Each segment is contiguous with the previous one:
- walk: `{k:"walk", s, e, anim:"walk", path:[[x,z],...], to}`
- other: `{k, s, e, anim, station, task, at:[x,z], face:[x,z]}` where `k` is one of work, wait, fetch, break, lunch, inspect, audit, observe, maintain, repair, pickup, standby, desk and `anim` is `walk`, `work`, `crouch` or `idle`.

Everyone starts at the gate and walks to their station. Breaks are staggered and covered by the relief operator. Station coverage is above 90 percent for every operated station. The technician response to the hero fault and the supervisor's reaction are scripted into shift A.

## sensors (parquet)
Columns: `t_s, machine_id, vibration_rms_mm_s, vib_bpfo_band, temperature_c, motor_current_a, load_pct, state, fault_label, fault_component, degradation`. `vib_bpfo_band` is energy at the bearing-defect frequency band and is the earliest indicator. `fault_label` is 1 once vibration would be at least 1.3x baseline; `degradation` is the 0 to 1 ground truth. States: running, idle, maintenance.

## Limits (be honest in the pitch)
Everything is synthetic and tuned by hand. Cycle times, noise levels and fault growth are plausible, not measured. The schedule is a scripted stand-in until the SimPy engine produces the same shapes live.
