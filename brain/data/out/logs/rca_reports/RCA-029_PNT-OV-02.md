# RCA-029: OVN-FAN-VIB: Recirculation fan high vibration

**Machine:** PNT-OV-02 (Cure Oven 2, Duerr Convection cure oven) - Area: Paint Shop
**Date:** 2025-12-19  **Shift:** A  **Downtime:** 301 min  **Estimated lost vehicles:** 159.8
**Work order:** WO-2025-009404  **Escalation level reached:** L6

## Problem statement
At 10:11 Cure Oven 2 went down with OVN-FAN-VIB. Operator tried reset twice, alarm returned.

## Containment
Line lead isolated the machine and rerouted material where possible. Maintenance supervisor was informed per the escalation matrix; vendor Duerr was engaged.

## Investigation
On site 10:26. Followed troubleshooting steps in the Duerr Convection cure oven manual for OVN-FAN-VIB. Measured vibration_mm_s = 5.03 mm/s (nominal 2.0, warn 5.0, alarm 8.0). Found: bearing wear.

## Root cause
Bearing wear (category: Wear / aging).

## Corrective actions taken
1) Vibration analysis done: measurement; clean impeller. 2) Replaced bearings; rebalance.

## Preventive actions
- Review PM interval for related tasks and add checklist step for early detection.
- Evaluate condition-monitoring sensor (current) alert threshold for earlier warning.
- Verify spare parts stock for the affected component.
- Share lessons learned at the weekly reliability meeting.
