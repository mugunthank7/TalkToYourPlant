# RCA-010: GP-VALVE-STICK: Servo/proportional valve sticking - position deviation

**Machine:** CAS-GP-01 (Giga Press 1, IDRA GIGAPRESS NEO 6100) - Area: Giga Casting & Machining
**Date:** 2025-01-25  **Shift:** C  **Downtime:** 338 min  **Estimated lost vehicles:** 129.5
**Work order:** WO-2025-000617  **Escalation level reached:** L6

## Problem statement
Giga Press 1 faulted (GP-VALVE-STICK). Line lead called maintenance via radio at 05:42.

## Containment
Line lead isolated the machine and rerouted material where possible. Maintenance supervisor was informed per the escalation matrix; vendor IDRA was engaged.

## Investigation
On site 05:46. Followed troubleshooting steps in the IDRA GIGAPRESS NEO 6100 manual for GP-VALVE-STICK. Measured vibration_mm_s = 4.86 mm/s (nominal 2.1, warn 5.0, alarm 8.0). Found: contaminated oil.

## Root cause
Contaminated oil (category: Contamination).

## Corrective actions taken
1) Flushed and filter oil. 2) Replaced valve; run valve calibration.

## Preventive actions
- Review PM interval for related tasks and add checklist step for early detection.
- Evaluate condition-monitoring sensor (vibration) alert threshold for earlier warning.
- Verify spare parts stock for the affected component.
- Share lessons learned at the weekly reliability meeting.
