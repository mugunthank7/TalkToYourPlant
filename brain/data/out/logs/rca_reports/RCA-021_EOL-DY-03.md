# RCA-021: CNC-SPINDLE-VIB: Spindle vibration above limit

**Machine:** EOL-DY-03 (Roll & Brake Dynamometer 3, MAHA Chassis dynamometer AWD) - Area: End-of-Line Test & Release
**Date:** 2025-06-23  **Shift:** A  **Downtime:** 310 min  **Estimated lost vehicles:** 130.8
**Work order:** WO-2025-004309  **Escalation level reached:** L6

## Problem statement
Roll & Brake Dynamometer 3 faulted (CNC-SPINDLE-VIB). Line lead called maintenance via radio at 13:44.

## Containment
Line lead isolated the machine and rerouted material where possible. Maintenance supervisor was informed per the escalation matrix; vendor MAHA was engaged.

## Investigation
Arrived 13:55. Checked tool balance and clamp force. Measured vibration_mm_s = 5.01 mm/s (nominal 1.5, warn 4.5, alarm 7.5). Cause: tool imbalance.

## Root cause
Tool imbalance (category: Unknown / under investigation).

## Corrective actions taken
Checked tool balance and clamp force.

## Preventive actions
- Review PM interval for related tasks and add checklist step for early detection.
- Evaluate condition-monitoring sensor (vibration) alert threshold for earlier warning.
- Verify spare parts stock for the affected component.
- Share lessons learned at the weekly reliability meeting.
