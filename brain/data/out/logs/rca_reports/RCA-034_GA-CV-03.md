# RCA-034: CNV-CHAIN-STRETCH: Chain elongation beyond limit

**Machine:** GA-CV-03 (Skillet/Overhead Conveyor 3, Duerr EcoProFlex skillet conveyor) - Area: General Assembly
**Date:** 2026-02-03  **Shift:** B  **Downtime:** 355 min  **Estimated lost vehicles:** 165.7
**Work order:** WO-2026-000945  **Escalation level reached:** L6

## Problem statement
Operator reports: chain elongation beyond limit. Machine halted in cycle, alarm CNV-CHAIN-STRETCH.

## Containment
Line lead isolated the machine and rerouted material where possible. Maintenance supervisor was informed per the escalation matrix; vendor Duerr was engaged.

## Investigation
On site 17:20. Followed troubleshooting steps in the Duerr EcoProFlex skillet conveyor manual for CNV-CHAIN-STRETCH. Measured vibration_mm_s = 5.25 mm/s (nominal 1.9, warn 5.0, alarm 8.0). Found: wear / lack of lubrication.

## Root cause
Wear / lack of lubrication (category: Lubrication).

## Corrective actions taken
1) Measured elongation (>3% replace).

## Preventive actions
- Review PM interval for related tasks and add checklist step for early detection.
- Evaluate condition-monitoring sensor (temperature) alert threshold for earlier warning.
- Verify spare parts stock for the affected component.
- Share lessons learned at the weekly reliability meeting.
