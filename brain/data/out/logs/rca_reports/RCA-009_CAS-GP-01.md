# RCA-009: GP-DIE-LEAK: Die cooling circuit water leak into die cavity

**Machine:** CAS-GP-01 (Giga Press 1, IDRA GIGAPRESS NEO 6100) - Area: Giga Casting & Machining
**Date:** 2025-01-14  **Shift:** B  **Downtime:** 387 min  **Estimated lost vehicles:** 160.4
**Work order:** WO-2025-000338  **Escalation level reached:** L6

## Problem statement
Operator reports: die cooling circuit water leak into die cavity. Machine halted in cycle, alarm GP-DIE-LEAK.

## Containment
Line lead isolated the machine and rerouted material where possible. Maintenance supervisor was informed per the escalation matrix; vendor IDRA was engaged.

## Investigation
Initial check 20:26, Giga Press 1 fault GP-DIE-LEAK confirmed. Probable cause cracked cooling insert.

## Root cause
Cracked cooling insert (category: Thermal / cooling).

## Corrective actions taken
Stopped cycle and secure die; pull die per SOP-CAS-02. Pressure-tested test circuits; replace insert.

## Preventive actions
- Review PM interval for related tasks and add checklist step for early detection.
- Evaluate condition-monitoring sensor (temperature) alert threshold for earlier warning.
- Verify spare parts stock for the affected component.
- Share lessons learned at the weekly reliability meeting.
