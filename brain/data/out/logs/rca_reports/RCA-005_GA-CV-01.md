# RCA-005: CNV-CHAIN-STRETCH: Chain elongation beyond limit

**Machine:** M06 (Main conveyor, Duerr EcoProFlex skillet conveyor) - Area: General Assembly
**Date:** 2024-12-04  **Shift:** A  **Downtime:** 283 min  **Estimated lost vehicles:** 141.6
**Work order:** WO-2024-001741  **Escalation level reached:** L6

## Problem statement
Alarm CNV-CHAIN-STRETCH - chain elongation beyond limit. Downstream line is blocked.

## Containment
Line lead isolated the machine and rerouted material where possible. Maintenance supervisor was informed per the escalation matrix; vendor Duerr was engaged.

## Investigation
Arrived 11:48. Measured elongation (>3% replace). Measured vibration_mm_s = 5.53 mm/s (nominal 1.9, warn 5.0, alarm 8.0). Cause: wear / lack of lubrication.

## Root cause
Wear / lack of lubrication (category: Lubrication).

## Corrective actions taken
Measured elongation (>3% replace).

## Preventive actions
- Review PM interval for related tasks and add checklist step for early detection.
- Evaluate condition-monitoring sensor (temperature) alert threshold for earlier warning.
- Verify spare parts stock for the affected component.
- Share lessons learned at the weekly reliability meeting.
