# RCA-057: OVN-FAN-VIB: Recirculation fan high vibration

**Machine:** PNT-ASU-02 (Air Supply Unit 2, Duerr EcoSupply) - Area: Paint Shop
**Date:** 2026-09-16  **Shift:** C  **Downtime:** 284 min  **Estimated lost vehicles:** 37.1
**Work order:** WO-2026-007276  **Escalation level reached:** L5

## Problem statement
Operator reports: recirculation fan high vibration. Machine halted in cycle, alarm OVN-FAN-VIB.

## Containment
Line lead isolated the machine and rerouted material where possible. Maintenance supervisor was informed per the escalation matrix; vendor Duerr was engaged.

## Investigation
Arrived 01:14. Vibration analysis done: measurement; clean impeller. Measured vibration_mm_s = 4.79 mm/s (nominal 2.2, warn 5.0, alarm 8.0). Cause: bearing wear.

## Root cause
Bearing wear (category: Wear / aging).

## Corrective actions taken
Replaced bearings; rebalance.

## Preventive actions
- Review PM interval for related tasks and add checklist step for early detection.
- Evaluate condition-monitoring sensor (temperature) alert threshold for earlier warning.
- Verify spare parts stock for the affected component.
- Share lessons learned at the weekly reliability meeting.
