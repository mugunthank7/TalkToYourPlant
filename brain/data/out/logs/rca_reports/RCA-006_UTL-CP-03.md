# RCA-006: CMP-MOTOR-OL: Main motor overload trip

**Machine:** UTL-CP-03 (Air Compressor 3, Atlas Copco GA 250 VSD+) - Area: Utilities & Facilities
**Date:** 2024-12-13  **Shift:** C  **Downtime:** 286 min  **Estimated lost vehicles:** 88.6
**Work order:** WO-2024-001954  **Escalation level reached:** L6

## Problem statement
Operator reports: main motor overload trip. Machine halted in cycle, alarm CMP-MOTOR-OL.

## Containment
Line lead isolated the machine and rerouted material where possible. Maintenance supervisor was informed per the escalation matrix; vendor Atlas Copco was engaged.

## Investigation
Initial check 02:43, Air Compressor 3 fault CMP-MOTOR-OL confirmed. Measured motor_current_a = 353.39 A (nominal 280, warn 350, alarm 390). Probable cause bearing failure.

## Root cause
Bearing failure (category: Wear / aging).

## Corrective actions taken
Checked motor insulation and bearing; check supply.

## Preventive actions
- Review PM interval for related tasks and add checklist step for early detection.
- Evaluate condition-monitoring sensor (vibration) alert threshold for earlier warning.
- Verify spare parts stock for the affected component.
- Share lessons learned at the weekly reliability meeting.
