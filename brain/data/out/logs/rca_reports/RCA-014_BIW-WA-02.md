**Date:** 2025-03-28  **Shift:** C  **Downtime:** 310 min  **Estimated lost vehicles:** 136.9
**Work order:** WO-2025-002127  **Escalation level reached:** L6

## Problem statement
Handling/Weld Robot (ABB) 2 faulted (ABB-GEAR-NOISE). Line lead called maintenance via radio at 00:46.

## Containment
Line lead isolated the machine and rerouted material where possible. Maintenance supervisor was informed per the escalation matrix; vendor ABB was engaged.

## Investigation
Arrived 00:49. Checked oil level and sample oil (Fe ppm). Measured vibration_mm_s = 4.79 mm/s (nominal 1.8, warn 4.5, alarm 7.1). Cause: low lubricant level.

## Root cause
Low lubricant level (category: Lubrication).

## Corrective actions taken
Vibration analysis done: analysis on gearbox.

## Preventive actions
