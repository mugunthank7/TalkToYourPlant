# RCA-052: GP-DIE-LEAK: Die cooling circuit water leak into die cavity

**Machine:** M01 (Casting press, IDRA GIGAPRESS NEO 6100) - Area: Giga Casting & Machining
**Date:** 2026-08-05  **Shift:** A  **Downtime:** 478 min  **Estimated lost vehicles:** 158.6
**Work order:** WO-2026-006126  **Escalation level reached:** L6

## Problem statement
GP-DIE-LEAK displayed on HMI; Casting press stopped at part pickup. Andon pulled at 06:52.

## Containment
Line lead isolated the machine and rerouted material where possible. Maintenance supervisor was informed per the escalation matrix; vendor IDRA was engaged.

## Investigation
Initial check 07:07, Casting press fault GP-DIE-LEAK confirmed. Probable cause o-ring failure.

## Root cause
O-ring failure (category: Unknown / under investigation).

## Corrective actions taken
Stopped cycle and secure die; pull die per SOP-CAS-02. Pressure-tested test circuits; replace insert.

## Preventive actions
- Review PM interval for related tasks and add checklist step for early detection.
- Evaluate condition-monitoring sensor (temperature) alert threshold for earlier warning.
- Verify spare parts stock for the affected component.
- Share lessons learned at the weekly reliability meeting.
