# RCA-040: OVN-FAN-VIB: Recirculation fan high vibration

**Machine:** PNT-OV-02 (Cure Oven 2, Duerr Convection cure oven) - Area: Paint Shop
**Date:** 2026-04-22  **Shift:** A  **Downtime:** 305 min  **Estimated lost vehicles:** 98.1
**Work order:** WO-2026-003097  **Escalation level reached:** L6

## Problem statement
OVN-FAN-VIB displayed on HMI; Cure Oven 2 stopped after a die/tool change. Andon pulled at 07:47.

## Containment
Line lead isolated the machine and rerouted material where possible. Maintenance supervisor was informed per the escalation matrix; vendor Duerr was engaged.

## Investigation
Arrived 07:58. Vibration analysis done: measurement; clean impeller. Measured vibration_mm_s = 5.60 mm/s (nominal 2.0, warn 5.0, alarm 8.0). Cause: bearing wear.

## Root cause
Bearing wear (category: Wear / aging).

## Corrective actions taken
Vibration analysis done: measurement; clean impeller.

## Preventive actions
- Review PM interval for related tasks and add checklist step for early detection.
- Evaluate condition-monitoring sensor (pressure) alert threshold for earlier warning.
- Verify spare parts stock for the affected component.
- Share lessons learned at the weekly reliability meeting.
