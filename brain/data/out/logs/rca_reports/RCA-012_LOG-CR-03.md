# RCA-012: CNV-CHAIN-STRETCH: Chain elongation beyond limit

**Machine:** LOG-CR-03 (Overhead Crane 3, Konecranes CXT 20 t) - Area: Intralogistics
**Date:** 2025-03-03  **Shift:** A  **Downtime:** 290 min  **Estimated lost vehicles:** 98.5
**Work order:** WO-2025-001545  **Escalation level reached:** L5

## Problem statement
Operator reports: chain elongation beyond limit. Machine halted in cycle, alarm CNV-CHAIN-STRETCH.

## Containment
Line lead isolated the machine and rerouted material where possible. Maintenance supervisor was informed per the escalation matrix; vendor Konecranes was engaged.

## Investigation
Arrived 09:10. Measured elongation (>3% replace). Measured vibration_mm_s = 4.76 mm/s (nominal 1.5, warn 4.5, alarm 7.0). Cause: wear / lack of lubrication.

## Root cause
Wear / lack of lubrication (category: Lubrication).

## Corrective actions taken
Lubricate; replace chain section.

## Preventive actions
- Review PM interval for related tasks and add checklist step for early detection.
- Evaluate condition-monitoring sensor (vibration) alert threshold for earlier warning.
- Verify spare parts stock for the affected component.
- Share lessons learned at the weekly reliability meeting.
