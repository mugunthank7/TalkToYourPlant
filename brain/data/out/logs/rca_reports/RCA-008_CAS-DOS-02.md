**Date:** 2025-01-13  **Shift:** B  **Downtime:** 396 min  **Estimated lost vehicles:** 200.4

## Problem statement
At 17:27 Dosing Furnace 2 went down with DOS-HEATER. Operator tried reset twice, alarm returned.

## Containment
Line lead isolated the machine and rerouted material where possible. Maintenance supervisor was informed per the escalation matrix; vendor Striko Westofen was engaged.

## Investigation
On site 17:38. Followed troubleshooting steps in the Striko Westofen Dosing furnace 12t holding manual for DOS-HEATER. Measured furnace_power_kw = 267.27 kW (nominal 210, warn 260, alarm 300). Found: dross attack.

## Root cause
Dross attack (category: Unknown / under investigation).

## Corrective actions taken
1) Replaced element; check refractory.
