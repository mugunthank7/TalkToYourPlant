# SOP-ENV-01: Oil and Fluid Spill Response

**Area:** ALL  |  **Owner:** EHS Officer  |  **Revision:** B  |  **Effective:** 2025-06-01

> **Synthetic training document.** Written for the Voltara Motors digital-twin demo. It is modeled on public OEM and industry information but it is NOT an OEM document. Items tagged `oem_public` were checked against public sources; items tagged `synthetic` are invented for realism. Do not use for real equipment.

1. Stop the leak source if safe (isolate, LOTO as needed).
2. Contain with absorbent socks/pads, protect drains.
3. Spills > 5 gallons or reaching a drain: notify EHS immediately.
4. Clean up, dispose in the labeled oily waste container, and record in the environmental log.
5. Open a work order to repair the source and review hose/fitting condition.
